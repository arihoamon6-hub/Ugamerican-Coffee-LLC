Uganda grows both species. We buy both.

Robusta is native to Uganda's forests, and the country is one of Africa's largest coffee exporters. High-altitude Arabica comes down from the Rwenzori and Elgon ranges. We work with washing stations in each region.

Arabica

Rwenzori Mountains

Washed, high-altitude lots from the slopes of the "Mountains of the Moon." Bright acidity, tea-like body.

Arabica

Mount Elgon

Volcanic soil on the Kenyan border. Fuller-bodied cups with stone fruit and brown sugar tones.

Robusta

West Nile

Uganda's native Robusta heartland. Deep, chocolatey, built for espresso and cold brew.

Robusta

Masaka & Central

Uganda's most-planted growing belt. Nutty, low-acid, an everyday cup with real body.
