Every bag makes the same trip

Ugamerican Coffee LLC was built to shorten the distance between Ugandan coffee farms and American coffee drinkers. Here's what the route actually looks like, start to finish.

Step 1

Cherry picked at the farm

We buy from smallholder cooperatives in the Rwenzori, Elgon, West Nile, and Central regions, paying above the local market rate at time of purchase.

Step 2 – 5

Processed, exported, roasted, and shipped

See the full process & roast guide

"Ugamerican" is the point: a coffee company with one foot in Uganda's growing regions and one in the American kitchens and cafés where the coffee ends up.

Uganda is one of Africa's largest coffee-producing countries, and the only place Robusta coffee grows wild — it's native to the country's forests. Arabica came later, planted at altitude in the Rwenzori and Elgon ranges, where cooler nights slow the cherry down and build more complex flavor.

We started this company to make that range of flavor easy to find in the U.S. — not folded into an anonymous "African blend," but sold lot by lot, region by region, with the price we paid for it printed on the bag.
